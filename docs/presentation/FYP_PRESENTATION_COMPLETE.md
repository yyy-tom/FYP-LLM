**Answer:** "Safety is about implementation, not size. We add custom crisis detection, control training data, and maintain human oversight. Self-hosting gives us MORE control than GPT-4 API."

**Answer:** "Strategic: 7B for deployment (fast, efficient), 14B for quality demonstration. Training both ($500) is negligible vs GPT-4 costs ($180K/year)."

## 🎨 Slide Design Tips

### Visual Consistency:
- **Color scheme:** Blue (Qwen), Red (GPT-4), Green (LLaMA)
- **Fonts:** Sans-serif, 24pt+ body, 36pt+ titles
- **Layout:** One main point per slide
- **Visuals:** Use generated PNGs (already 300 DPI)

### Content Balance:
- **Title slides:** Minimal text, strong visual
- **Data slides:** Chart + 3-5 bullet points
- **Comparison slides:** Side-by-side or overlaid visuals
- **Summary slides:** Key takeaways + visual metaphor

### Avoid:
- ❌ Overcrowded slides (max 5 bullet points)
- ❌ Small fonts (<20pt body text)
- ❌ Low-contrast colors (light text on light background)
- ❌ Too many animations (distracting)
- ❌ Reading slides verbatim (engage audience)
